Who are you, legally?

Nuvra Solutions Ltd., registered in England and Wales, company number 16155976, registered office 130 Birmingham New Road, Wolverhampton WV4 6NY — all verifiable at Companies House.

Do you have a shop I can visit?

No. We sell direct and online rather than from a retail shop, which is what keeps the pricing where it is.

Can I cancel an order?

If you're buying as a consumer, yes — 14 days from delivery for most orders under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, and we don't make it difficult.

What if something arrives faulty?

You have separate rights for faulty or misdescribed goods. Email us and we'll sort it rather than quoting policy.
